Cinematakis

The cinema that comes to you

A mobile cinema from Tsintzina, Laconia. We set up our screen in village squares and courtyards, and watch films together under the stars.

See upcoming screenings

How it works

  • Mobile

    We have no fixed venue. Every screening takes place in a different location.

  • Pop-up

    Screenings happen from time to time. The schedule is announced on the Events page.

  • Free admission

    For now, all screenings are free for everyone.

Why “Cinematakis”?

The name combines “cinema” and “Takis”, the nickname of our founder.
